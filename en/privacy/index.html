Information you give us. When you fill out our contact form, message us on WhatsApp, email us, call us or book a call, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the message you send and anything else you choose to share, such as your business name and goals.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Site, we and our partners collect technical and usage data through cookies, pixels and similar technologies. Payment information. If you hire us, payments are processed by Stripe, PayPal or Mercado Pago under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store your full card number. We may receive confirmation of payment and basic transaction details.

Information from advertising and analytics partners. Platforms such as Google, Meta, TikTok and Microsoft may give us aggregated or limited information about how visitors reached the Site and responded to our ads.

Your choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
